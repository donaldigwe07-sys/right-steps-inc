About Susana Homes

A Place Where Every Child Matters

Susana Homes is an orphanage home under Right Steps Inc., established to provide a caring and supportive environment for vulnerable children.

We believe that every child deserves love, protection, dignity, education and the opportunity to grow into a responsible and confident individual.

Through compassionate care and guidance, Susana Homes seeks to give children a sense of belonging and hope for a better future.

Our commitment: To provide a safe, loving and supportive environment where children can grow, learn and thrive.
What Guides Us

Our Mission and Vision

We are committed to making a positive difference in the lives of children through love, care, guidance and support.

Our Mission

To provide vulnerable children with a safe and caring environment where they can receive support, develop their abilities and build a foundation for a better life.

Our Vision

To see every child grow in dignity, confidence, knowledge and character, with the opportunity to become a productive and responsible member of society.

Our Commitment to Children

What We Do at Susana Homes

We believe that caring for a child involves more than providing a roof over their head. It means supporting their overall growth and well-being.

Safe Shelter

Providing a safe and supportive home environment where children can feel protected, welcomed and valued.

Basic Needs

Supporting children's daily needs, including food, clothing, personal care and a healthy living environment.

Education

Encouraging learning and personal development so that every child has the opportunity to build a brighter future.

Love and Care

Creating a caring atmosphere where children receive encouragement, attention and emotional support.

Spiritual Development

Encouraging good character, moral values and spiritual growth in accordance with the Christian principles of Right Steps Inc.

Hope for the Future

Helping children develop confidence, discover their abilities and prepare for meaningful and independent lives.

Our Beginning

The Beginning of Right Steps Inc.

Susana Homes holds a special place in the story of Right Steps Inc.

Susana Homes was the first establishment under Right Steps Inc. It represents an important part of the organisation's foundation and its commitment to making a positive difference in people's lives.

From this beginning, Right Steps Inc. has a story that connects care for vulnerable children with its broader commitment to education, personal development and service.

Susana Homes remains an important part of this story, representing the value of compassion and the belief that every child deserves the opportunity to experience a better future.
