About Our Church

A Christian community committed to worship, biblical teaching, spiritual growth, and service.

Welcome to Our Church Family

The Church of Christ @ Susana Homes is a Christian congregation committed to the worship of God and the teaching of the Word of God.

Our desire is to encourage people to know Jesus Christ, grow in their understanding of Scripture, develop godly character, and demonstrate Christian love in their daily lives.

We believe that the church should be a place where people can learn, pray, fellowship, encourage one another, and serve God together.

Our Commitment

We are committed to biblical teaching, sincere worship, Christian fellowship, prayer, and practical service to others.

Our Mission & Vision

Seeking to honour God and encourage Christ-centred living through faith, fellowship, and service.

Our Mission

To encourage people to understand and follow the teachings of Jesus Christ, grow in the knowledge of God's Word, worship God faithfully, and serve others with love and compassion.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centred congregation where people grow spiritually, strengthen one another in faith, and become positive influences in their families and communities.

Our Church Activities

Opportunities for worship, learning, fellowship, prayer, and Christian service.

Biblical Teaching

Learning from the Bible and applying its teachings to our everyday lives.

Prayer & Worship

Coming together to pray, worship God, and strengthen our faith in Him.

Christian Fellowship

Building meaningful relationships and encouraging one another in Christian living.

Care & Compassion

Demonstrating Christian love through kindness, encouragement, and care for others.

Praise & Thanksgiving

Giving thanks to God and celebrating His goodness through praise and worship.

Spiritual Growth

Encouraging individuals and families to develop faith, godly character, and Christ-centred values.
