Who We Are

About Ella Lee Farms

Ella Lee Farms is the agricultural arm of Right Steps Inc., established to engage in farming, animal rearing, and crop production.

Our focus is on agriculture as a valuable means of producing food, developing practical farming skills, and contributing to the well-being of our communities.

Through crop cultivation and livestock management, Ella Lee Farms represents our commitment to agricultural productivity, responsible resource use, and growth.

  • Crop cultivation and agricultural production
  • Livestock and animal rearing
  • Responsible farm management
  • Agricultural development and productivity
