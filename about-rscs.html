ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Welcome to RSCS

Right Steps Christian Schools (RSCS) is an educational ministry under Right Steps Inc. committed to providing education in an environment that values learning, discipline, personal development, and Christian principles.

We believe that education should prepare students not only for academic success but also for responsible living, service to others, and a brighter future.

  • Commitment to quality education
  • Emphasis on character and discipline
  • Encouragement of personal growth
  • Education guided by Christian values
