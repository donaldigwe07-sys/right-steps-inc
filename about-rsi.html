WHO WE ARE

Taking the Right Steps Towards a Better Future

Right Steps Inc. is an organisation whose ministries focus on education, Christian living, care for vulnerable children and other community-oriented activities.

Through its various ministries and institutions, Right Steps Inc. seeks to provide opportunities for learning, spiritual growth, personal development and service to the community.

Guided by Christian principles, the organisation aims to encourage individuals to develop their abilities, build positive values and make meaningful contributions to society.

Our guiding principle is to make a positive difference through faith, education, care and service.
