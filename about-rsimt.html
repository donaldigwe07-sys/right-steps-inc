About RSIMT

Right Steps Institute of Management and Technology is an educational institution under Right Steps Inc., committed to providing quality education, practical skills, and character development for a better future.

Right Steps Institute of Management and Technology

Right Steps Institute of Management and Technology (RSIMT) is an educational institute established under Right Steps Inc. with the aim of providing students with quality education, professional knowledge, practical skills, and strong moral values.

RSIMT is committed to preparing students to become knowledgeable, skilled, responsible, and productive members of society. Through education and practical training, the institute seeks to equip students with the skills required to succeed in their chosen fields.

As part of the Right Steps Inc. family, RSIMT combines academic development with character formation and Christian values, encouraging students to pursue excellence while making a positive impact in their communities.

Our Mission & Vision

What guides RSIMT in educating and developing students.

Our Mission

To provide accessible and quality education that develops students academically, professionally, morally, and spiritually while equipping them with practical skills for meaningful contribution to society.

Our Vision

To become a respected centre of learning known for academic excellence, practical education, innovation, character development, and the preparation of students for successful and purposeful lives.

Our Core Values

The principles that shape the RSIMT community.

Excellence

We encourage students and staff to pursue excellence in learning, teaching, and service.

Integrity

We promote honesty, responsibility, discipline, and accountability.

Innovation

We encourage creativity, critical thinking, technology, and practical problem-solving.

Service

We encourage students to use their knowledge and skills to positively impact their communities.

What RSIMT Offers
