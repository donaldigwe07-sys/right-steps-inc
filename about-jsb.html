About JSB Institute

A place for biblical learning, spiritual development, and preparation for Christian ministry.

Training Ministers. Transforming Lives.

The JSB Institute of Biblical Studies and Christian Living Centre is a Bible college dedicated to training and equipping ministers of God for effective Christian service.

The Institute focuses on helping students understand the Word of God, strengthen their faith in Jesus Christ, and develop the character and discipline required for ministry.

Through biblical instruction and spiritual development, students are encouraged to become responsible servants of God who can teach the Scriptures, support their communities, and share the message of the Gospel.

Our Slogan

"A Christian Living Centre" expresses the Institute's commitment to biblical understanding and a Christ-centred life.

Our Mission & Vision

Building a strong foundation for Christian faith and effective ministry.

Our Mission

To equip men and women with sound biblical knowledge, Christian values, and the spiritual preparation necessary for faithful service to God and meaningful service to humanity.

Our Vision

To develop spiritually mature and biblically grounded ministers who will share the Gospel, demonstrate Christ-like character, and positively influence their churches and communities.
