OUR GOALS

Right Steps Inc is dedicated to helping people move forward with confidence. We provide programs and resources that build skills, create opportunities, and develop individuals for the near future. Our mission is simple: guide every person to take the right step toward a brighter future.

Education & Training

We as an organisation are aimed at supporting childrens dreames by giving thier Mentorships and skill development programs.This is our core mission.

Community Support

We provide Outreach programs that connect people to resources and support network. we also offer nutritional support to the children of the community that need it.Community support is important because it helps people feel valued and connected. When people support one another, they are less likely to feel alone when facing difficulties. It also makes communities safer, stronger, and more united. For example, neighbours can work together to keep their environment clean, while students can help classmates who are struggling with schoolwork.Another important benefit of community support is that it encourages kindness and cooperation. When people help others, they create a positive environment where everyone has a chance to succeed. Young people can also learn important values such as responsibility, empathy, respect, and teamwork. In conclusion, community support plays an important role in building a strong and caring society. By helping one another and working together, people can overcome challenges and create a better community for everyone.

Career Guidance
